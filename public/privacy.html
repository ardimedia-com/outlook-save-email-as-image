Privacy Policy

Save Email as Image — an Outlook add-in by Ardimedia

Last updated: 28 May 2026

In short

This add-in processes everything locally in your browser and does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Your email content never leaves your device, and our servers receive none of it.

What the add-in does

Save Email as Image renders the email you currently have open in Outlook into a PNG or JPG image so you can save it or copy it to your clipboard. All rendering happens inside the add-in task pane (a sandboxed browser frame) on your own device.

Data we access

  • The content and metadata (sender, recipients, subject, date) of the currently open message — read only, and only while you use the add-in. This requires the minimal ReadItem permission.
  • Inline images embedded in that message, to include them in the exported image.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No email content, addresses, subjects, or attachments are sent anywhere.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, cookies, or advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, no profiles, no usage logs on our side.

Where processing happens

Image generation runs entirely client-side using your browser's canvas. The web server that hosts the add-in serves only static, public files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS, icons, fonts). It has no backend and receives no message data.

External images

By default, external (remote) images in an email are blocked and shown as placeholders — the same protective behaviour as Outlook. If you explicitly choose “load external images for this export”, your browser fetches those images directly from their third-party hosts (which may record the request, as with any remote image). This is your choice and is off by default.

Fonts

The interface font is bundled with the add-in and served from our own host — no third-party font CDN is used, so no font request is made to external providers.

Third parties

The add-in loads Microsoft's Office.js library from Microsoft's official CDN, as required for all Office add-ins. We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Open source

The add-in is open source under the MIT license. You can review exactly what it does at github.com/ardimedia-com/outlook-save-email-as-image.
